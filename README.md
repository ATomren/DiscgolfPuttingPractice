# Putt Log

A simple disc golf putting tracker for practice sessions. Log your putts with one tap, watch your hit rate per zone, and follow your progress over time.

Open it here: https://atomren.github.io/DiscgolfPuttingPractice/

## Install on your phone
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

It then opens like a normal app and works offline.

Note for iPhone: the home screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari. If you already logged putts in Safari, export a backup there and import it in the installed app (see Backup below).

## How to use
**Log**
1. Tap the disc count button (top right) and pick how many discs you throw per round.
2. Set the distance with the + and - buttons (4 to 40 m).
3. After each round, tap how many went in. That's it.
Made a mistake? Tap Undo in the message that pops up, or the x next to a round in the list.

**Today** shows today's putts, hit rate per zone, and hit rate per distance.

**Trends** shows your progress:
- A goal you choose (a zone or a distance, plus a target hit rate) with a progress bar.
- A chart of your hit rate per practice day.
- "Where you stand": your latest day compared with your earlier putts.

**Zones:** C1X is up to 10 m, C2 is 10 to 20 m, Outside is beyond 20 m.

## Backup
Your putts are saved on your phone only. Go to Settings and tap "Export backup" now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe (email it to yourself, for example). To restore, use Restore in Settings with that file. Restoring never duplicates rounds you already have.

## Cloud sync (optional, in testing)
The cloud version at https://atomren.github.io/DiscgolfPuttingPractice/cloud.html lets you sign in with Google or email, so your putts are backed up and can be used on more than one device. Signing in is optional.

Privacy: your putts and email are stored in Google Firebase (EU). Passwords are handled by Firebase and never visible to the app owner. The app owner can technically see stored stats and emails but does not look at them. You can delete your cloud data and account from Settings at any time.
